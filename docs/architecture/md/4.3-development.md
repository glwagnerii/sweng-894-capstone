## 4.3 Development Viewpoint Definition

### 4.3.1 Abstract
The development view focuses on the organization of software modules and components within the Classifi-Cam system. It describes the architecture from the perspective of the development team, including module structure, dependencies, and the build and deployment process. This view supports maintainability, extensibility, and effective collaboration among team members.

### 4.3.2 Stakeholders and Their Concerns Addressed

This view addresses the concerns of the following stakeholders:
- **Software Developers**: Understand module structure and dependencies to guide implementation and integration.
- **DevOps Engineers**: Ensure build and deployment processes are efficient, reliable, and support CI/CD.
- **Test Engineers**: Verify modular design supports extensibility and maintainability.
- **Configuration Managers**: Manage versioning, branching, and merging of source code and artifacts.

### 4.3.3 Elements, Relations, and Properties
Key elements in the development view include:
- **Software Modules**: Logical groupings of source code, configuration files, and artifacts implementing specific functionality.
- **Dependencies**: Relationships between modules, such as import/export of interfaces, shared libraries, or configuration data.
- **Build Artifacts**: Deployable units produced by the build process (e.g., executables, libraries, container images).
- **Build and Deployment Processes**: Automated workflows for compiling, packaging, and deploying the Classifi-Cam system.

Relationships capture the hierarchical structure of modules, dependencies, and the flow of build and deployment processes. Architectural styles include:
- **Layered**: Organizes the system into distinct layers (e.g., presentation, business logic, data access) to promote separation of concerns.
- **Aspect-Oriented**: Modularizes cross-cutting concerns (e.g., logging, error handling, configuration).

### 4.3.4 Language(s) to Model/Represent Conforming Views
The development view will be represented using:
- **UML Component Diagrams**: Illustrate module structure and dependencies.
- **UML Deployment Diagrams**: Show build artifacts and their deployment.
- **UML Sequence Diagrams**: Depict build and deployment processes.

### 4.3.5 Applicable Evaluation/Analysis Techniques and Consistency/Completeness Criteria
The development view will be evaluated for:
- **Modularity and Cohesion**: Degree of separation of concerns and coherence of modules.
- **Dependency Management**: Handling of module dependencies, version compatibility, and conflict resolution.
- **Build and Deployment Automation**: Efficiency, reliability, and traceability of build and deployment processes.
- **Architectural Style Adherence**: Application of layered and aspect-oriented styles.
- **Consistency**: Alignment with requirements and other viewpoints (e.g., logical, physical).
- **Traceability**: Mapping requirements to modules and artifacts.
- **Peer Review/Automated Analysis**: Validation through stakeholder walkthroughs and analysis tools.
